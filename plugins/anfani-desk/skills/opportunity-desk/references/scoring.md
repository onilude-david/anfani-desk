# Gates and score

## Hard gates (fail any one = Ineligible, score 0)

The user's own values for each gate live in `desk/gates.md` (written at setup). Check each candidate against them.

1. **Nationality or residence** outside the call (for example EU-only, US-only, one region only).
2. **Age** outside the limit on the programme's reference date (read the rule: on the deadline, at the start date, on
   1 January, "under 30" meaning up to the 30th birthday). Watch birthdays that fall inside the window.
3. **Stage:** student-only or graduate-only calls that do not match; revenue, funding or team-size floors not met.
4. **Degree and grades:** minimum class or GPA (for example a 2:1), degree subject, years since graduating.
5. **Registration and documents:** registered organisation, audited accounts, years of operation, tax or
   anti-money-laundering certificates, if the call requires them and the user does not have them.
6. **Location and time:** in-person programmes the user cannot attend for the full run, unless travel is funded;
   visa rules for the user's nationality.
7. **Sector or theme** the user is not in, unless a matching track exists.
8. **Money:** pay-to-apply above a token fee, pay-to-attend "scholarships" that are discounts on a ticket, pay-to-win
   schemes, or anything that looks like a vanity award.

**Vanity-risk flag (not a gate):** cash paid only on completion by an organisation with no verifiable past cohorts,
alumni or partners. Score it, flag it at the top of the sheet, and confirm the organisation is real first.

## Score (0–10 for everything that passes the gates)

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value (money, visibility, access, credential) | small | useful | changes the year |
| Evidence (how much of the ask is already in the user's record) | weak | partial | strong |
| Strategic fit (the user's stated goals) | off to the side | adjacent | on the line |
| Effort against time (effort band vs days to deadline, documents from others) | tight or very large | fair | easy |
| Odds (selectivity; whether the record matches past winners) | long shot | fair | good |

**Priority bonus:** +2 for the user's top priority category and for anything that builds evidence for their top goal
(for example a judging seat or a recorded talk when they are building a talent-visa file); +1 for their next two
categories; 0 for the rest. Set from `desk/priorities.md`. The bonus orders the shortlist and decides which sheets get
filled first; it never lifts anything past a hard gate.

7+ (before the bonus) shortlist. 5–6 note for next cycle. Under 5 skip, with one line why. Order the shortlist by score
plus bonus, then deadline. Effort bands: S under an hour, M an evening, L a weekend, XL needs documents from others.
